Hao Zhou is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the University of Texas at Dallas. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Pennsylvania State University. His research focuses on AI-powered mobile and wearable systems, time-series representation learning, and low-cost sensing for healthcare and accessibility. During the final year of his Ph.D., he worked with the Digital Health Lab at Samsung Research America, where he developed wearable foundation models for multimodal sensor data. He has also conducted research at Microsoft Research Asia. His work has received several other honors, including the Penn State Alumni Association Dissertation Award, an IoTDI Best Paper Award, and selection as an ACM MobiSys Rising Star.
