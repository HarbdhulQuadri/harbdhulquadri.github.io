    Postgraduate Research & Publications

    A Dual-Teacher Adaptive Agreement Strategy for Semi-Supervised Federated Learning in Abdominal Trauma CT Segmentation
    M.Sc. Thesis (In View)
    University of Lagos · Department of Computer Sciences · Supervised by Dr. F. O. Alamu (Matric: 259074260)
    In acute clinical emergency situations such as abdominal trauma, 3D CT segmentation is essential for rapid identification of internal organ lacerations and hemorrhages. Strict privacy regulations (HIPAA, GDPR) prohibit centralizing sensitive clinical datasets across multi-hospital silos. This thesis introduces a Dual-Teacher Adaptive Agreement (DTAA) framework under Federated Semi-Supervised Learning (FSSL), enabling distributed clinical nodes with sparse local ground-truth labels to collaboratively train robust 3D segmentation models without exposing raw patient scans.
    Federated Learning Semi-Supervised Learning (FSSL) 3D CT Segmentation Abdominal Trauma Healthcare Privacy
    Federated Learning in Healthcare: Privacy-Preserving Collaborative Machine Learning Across Clinical Silos
    M.Sc. Seminar (CSC 812)
    University of Lagos · Department of Computer Sciences · Supervised by Dr. F. O. Alamu
    Comprehensive postgraduate seminar examining decentralized machine learning paradigms in healthcare. Analyzes horizontal and vertical FL topologies, differential privacy, secure multi-party aggregation, and clinical non-IID data distribution bottlenecks across hospital institutions.
