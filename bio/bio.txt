Abdulquadri Adegbiji is a Senior Backend Engineer, Cloud Systems Architect, and Researcher based in Nigeria. He is currently pursuing his Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Computer Science (in view) at the University of Lagos, specializing in Federated Learning and Healthcare AI under the supervision of Dr. F. O. Alamu, and holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Ekiti State University. With over five years of professional experience across distributed systems, fintech, logistics, and intelligent automation, he specializes in architecting high-throughput event-driven data ingestion pipelines (Python, FastAPI, Node.js, TypeScript), designing resilient PostgreSQL schemas, and orchestrating containerized cloud environments on AWS. Beyond core engineering, Abdulquadri has mentored over 500 engineers in backend systems and data science, authored open-source developer tooling (create-quadstack on NPM), and conducts research on decentralized privacy-preserving deep learning.
