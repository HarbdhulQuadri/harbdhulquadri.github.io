Abdulquadri Adegbiji is a Senior Backend Engineer, Cloud Systems Architect, and Researcher based in Nigeria. He holds a Master of Science in Computer Science from the University of Lagos and a Bachelor of Science (Ed.) in Computer Science Education from Ekiti State University. With over five years of professional experience across distributed systems, fintech, and intelligent automation, he specializes in architecting event-driven data ingestion pipelines (Python, FastAPI, Node.js, TypeScript), designing scalable database schemas, and orchestrating containerized cloud environments on AWS. Beyond engineering, Abdulquadri has mentored over 500 engineers and students in backend systems and data science, and co-authored peer-reviewed research in applied machine learning for clinical diagnostics.
